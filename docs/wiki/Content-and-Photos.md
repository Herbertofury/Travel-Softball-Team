# Content and photos

`site.config.js` is the single content source. Change the brand, story, roster, schedule, gallery, sponsors, and contact there. The [content guide](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Travel-Softball-Team/blob/main/docs/CONTENT-GUIDE.md) maps every field.

Replace the three optimized local photographs in `assets/images/`. The supplied photos are illustrative softball photography, not photographs of the concept team. Their credits and reuse terms are in [PHOTO-CREDITS.json](https://github.com/Herbertofury/Travel-Softball-Team/blob/main/docs/PHOTO-CREDITS.json).

Publish only approved player information. Leave optional contact, profile, and sponsor URLs empty until a real destination is available. Set `preview: false` after replacing the illustrative data.
