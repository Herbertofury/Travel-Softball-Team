PRIVACY.

Team contact

Trent is the team owner and contact for this website and app. Questions or data requests: trent@kerrpanel.com or 720-292-6603.

What is saved

The calendar and team information are public. RSVP responses contain the player/family name you enter, attendance choice, guest count, optional note, update time, and a site-specific account identifier. ChatGPT sign-in supplies that identifier and an email for authorized phone connections and schedule-management permissions. The app does not receive or store your ChatGPT password.

Who can see it

Public visitors see aggregate attendance totals. Your name and note are visible to you and authorized team schedule managers, including Trent and the site’s technical administrator. Event details are public. Avoid birth dates, medical information, home addresses, or other sensitive player details in notes.

Phone storage & reminders

The app can keep a public schedule snapshot on your device for offline viewing. Private RSVP notes are not included in that snapshot. Native phone sign-in uses encrypted iOS Keychain or Android Keystore storage, with a revocable session that expires after 30 days. Event reminders are optional local phone notifications; notification permission is requested when you choose a reminder. The app does not use location tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

Service providers

Sites hosting and ChatGPT sign-in provide the website and identity services. Cloudflare D1 stores schedule edits, attendance, and app connection/session records. Native platform features use Capacitor and its installed plugins. Requests needed to deliver these services may be processed and logged by hosting, authentication, and app-store providers under their own terms.

Retention & deletion

Attendance records remain until you remove individual responses or delete all your attendance data. Schedule managers can retain event history. Pending phone connections expire after five minutes; expired connection and session records are cleaned up during later phone sign-ins. Use Account & data in the app or the page below to delete your attendance records and revoke your app sessions. Deleting team-app data does not delete your ChatGPT account.
